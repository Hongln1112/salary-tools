Why these tools help

Understanding your pay — whether you are an employee, a freelancer, or someone negotiating a job offer — comes down to a few simple numbers: your hourly rate, how many hours you work, and what that adds up to over a year. These calculators do the math instantly so you can make clear, informed decisions about your money.

Pay & salary FAQs

At a standard 40-hour, 52-week schedule, $50,000 a year is about $24.04 per hour before taxes.

Gross pay is before any deductions; net (take-home) pay is what you keep after federal, state and FICA taxes. Use the Take-Home Pay Calculator to go from gross to net.

Divide your annual salary by your total hours per year (hours per week × weeks per year). For 40 hours × 52 weeks, that's 2,080 hours.

Under federal law, overtime is 1.5x your regular rate for hours beyond 40 in a week. The Overtime Calculator handles time-and-a-half and double-time.
