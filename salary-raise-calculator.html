What's a typical raise?

In the US, average annual merit raises run 3–5% for cost-of-living adjustments, and 5–10% for promotions or significant performance improvements. Anything above 10% usually comes with a new title or significantly expanded responsibilities. Job switching typically yields 10–20% higher pay than staying at the same company.
