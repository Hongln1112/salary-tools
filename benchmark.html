How to read this

The US average hourly wage across all industries is roughly $29.60, and the median (where half earn less, half earn more) is about $26.50. These are gross figures — before taxes. Your effective take-home hourly rate will be 20–35% lower depending on your state and filing status.

City averages adjust for cost of living and industry mix. A $30/hour job in San Francisco is a middle-income salary there, but the same rate in Dallas or Atlanta puts you well above the local average.

Figures are approximate 2026 estimates based on BLS and public wage data. Individual salaries vary significantly by industry, education, experience, and negotiation skill. This is a general reference, not personalized compensation advice.
