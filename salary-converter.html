How the conversion works

Annual = Hourly × Hours/week × Weeks/year

From that single relationship, every other figure follows: Weekly = Annual ÷ 52, and Monthly = Annual ÷ 12. Pick whichever number you already know, and the converter fills in the rest.

Example: $25/hour, 40 hours a week and 52 weeks a year equals about $52,000 a year, $1,000 a week, and $4,333 a month.

This tool converts gross pay between time periods on a simple basis and does not account for taxes, deductions or benefits. Monthly figures are annual ÷ 12 for simplicity.
