How minimum wage works in the US

The federal minimum wage has been $7.25/hour since 2009. Many states set a higher minimum wage — when a state rate is higher than the federal rate, employers must pay the higher one.

  • Federal floor: $7.25/hour (2026)
  • State minimums: range from $7.25 to over $17/hour, and many adjust yearly for inflation
  • Cities like Seattle and New York City can set even higher local minimums (e.g. Seattle's $20.76)

What if my pay is below the minimum?

If your calculated hourly rate falls below the minimum for your state, that may mean your pay is not compliant. You should speak with your employer or your state's labor department — exceptions apply to certain job categories, but most non-exempt workers are covered.

Is minimum wage per month or per year?

Minimum wage is stated per hour. To work out the annual minimum, multiply the hourly rate by 2,080 hours (40 hours/week × 52 weeks). For example, $15/hour × 2,080 = $31,200/year.
