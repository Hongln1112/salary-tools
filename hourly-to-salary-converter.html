FAQ

Multiply your hourly rate by 40 (hours per week), then by 52 (weeks per year). Example: $20/hr × 40 × 52 = $41,600/year.

52 is standard. If you get 2 weeks of unpaid time off, use 50. If you get paid vacation, keep 52 — you're still earning during those weeks.

$20/hr = $41,600/year. That's above the US federal poverty line for a household of two, but tight in expensive cities like San Francisco or New York. In most of the country, it's a decent entry-level wage.

This calculator provides estimates for general guidance only. Actual take-home pay depends on taxes, deductions, overtime, and benefits. Always confirm your pay details with your employer.
