What is take-home pay?

Take-home pay (or net pay) is the money left after federal income tax, FICA (Social Security + Medicare), and state income tax are withheld from your gross salary. It is what actually lands in your bank account.

Take-home = Gross − Federal − FICA − State

In 2026, FICA is 7.65% of wages (6.2% Social Security up to $184,500, plus 1.45% Medicare). The standard deduction is $16,100 for single filers, $32,200 for married filing jointly, and $24,150 for head of household.

This calculator applies the standard deduction and 2026 federal brackets, then subtracts FICA and an estimated flat state rate. It is a solid estimate — not a substitute for a tax professional.
