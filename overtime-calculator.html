How overtime pay works

Under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA), most hourly employees earn 1.5 times their regular rate for hours worked beyond 40 in a workweek — this is called time-and-a-half. Some jobs, holidays or agreements use double time (2×) instead.

Weekly pay = (Regular hours × Rate) + (Overtime hours × Rate × Multiplier)

Example: a $20/hour rate, 40 regular hours and 5 overtime hours at 1.5× gives $950 that week ($800 regular + $150 overtime).

This tool estimates gross weekly pay and does not account for taxes, deductions, or state-specific overtime rules. Some states have additional overtime laws. Verify with your employer or state labor department.
