How commission pay works

Sales commission is a percentage of the sales you make, paid on top of (or instead of) a base salary. Common structures include straight commission (no base), base + commission, and tiered commission (higher rates as you hit bigger targets).

Our calculator uses the simplest model: a flat commission rate on your total sales, plus any base pay you earn in the same period.

Common commission rates

Retail sales: 3–7%
Real estate agent: 2.5–3% per side
Insurance sales: 10–50% of first-year premium
B2B SaaS sales: 5–15% of monthly recurring revenue
