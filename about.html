About Salary & Pay Tools

Free, private, browser-based pay calculators.

What we do

Salary & Pay Tools is a free collection of salary, wage, overtime and take-home pay calculators. Our goal is simple: help you understand your pay — hourly rate, annual income, overtime earnings, and what you actually keep after taxes — without a spreadsheet and without signing up.

How our numbers are calculated

Every tool uses standard, transparent formulas so you can reproduce any result yourself:

  • Hourly rate — annual salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year), the standard full-time convention of 2,080 hours a year.
  • Salary conversion — direct conversion between hourly, weekly, monthly and annual figures (monthly uses annual ÷ 12).
  • Overtime — regular pay for up to 40 hours plus 1.5x for overtime hours (time-and-a-half), or 2x where double-time is chosen.
  • Take-home pay — gross salary minus federal income tax (2026 progressive brackets and standard deduction), minus FICA (6.2% Social Security up to the wage base plus 1.45% Medicare), minus estimated state tax.

Where a number depends on your situation — your state's exact tax brackets, your employer, your W-4 — we say so and point you to the right source, rather than pretending one figure fits everyone.

Data sources

  • Federal income tax — 2026 federal brackets and standard deductions (IRS).
  • FICA rates and wage base — Social Security and Medicare (SSA / IRS).
  • State income tax — state tax departments; flat rates where a state uses one, and typical effective rates where a state uses progressive brackets.
  • Minimum wage — U.S. Department of Labor and individual state minimum wage data.

Editorial & update process

  • Tax rates and minimum wages are reviewed and updated as official figures change (the take-home tool tracks 2026 rates).
  • We keep the calculators deliberately simple and label every estimate as an estimate.
  • If a rule varies by state or employer, the page tells you to verify with the relevant authority.

Why you can trust us

  • Free, private and transparent. Everything runs in your browser — no sign-up, no data collection, no hidden fees.
  • No sales agenda. We don't push any bank, employer or product. The math is neutral.
  • Clear boundaries. We're explicit about what's general education vs. personal financial advice, and we always recommend confirming with a qualified professional.

A note on accuracy

These tools give estimates using standard formulas and published rates. Real paychecks and tax withholding depend on your W-4, employer, benefits, and local rules. Treat the results as guidance and confirm with a qualified professional before making financial decisions.
