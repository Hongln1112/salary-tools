About Salary & Pay Tools

We built these calculators because figuring out your pay shouldn't require a spreadsheet. Whether you're negotiating a job offer, comparing hourly vs. salary, or just want to know what your paycheck actually means, these tools give you the answer in seconds.

All calculations run locally in your browser. We don't store your numbers, ask for your email, or make you sign up. Free, fast, and private.
