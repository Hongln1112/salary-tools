Why freelancers need a different rate formula

As a freelancer or contractor, your hourly rate needs to cover far more than an employee's salary. You must factor in:

  • Taxes — you pay both the employee and employer side of FICA (self-employment tax).
  • Expenses — software, tools, home office, health insurance, accounting.
  • Vacation & sick leave — you don't get paid time off, so you must bill for it.
  • Non-billable hours — emails, marketing, admin don't earn money.

Example

If you want $60,000 take-home, pay 25% tax, spend $5,000 on expenses, bill 25 hours/week and take 4 weeks vacation, your required rate works out to roughly $90–$100/hour. Employees often earn the equivalent of $40–$50/hour — freelancers charge more because they bear all these costs.
